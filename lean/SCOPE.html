Formal scope for manuscript v6.4

Compilation certifies the statements encoded in the sources. Scope labels below distinguish finite support from general history measures, actual quantizers from prescribed errors, and algebraic hypotheses from derived expectations.

A minimal reading anchor: fixed-error residual identities hold the new error fixed while strength changes. An actual nearest quantizer generally changes that error. Similarly, a bilinear commutator bound and an abstract recurrence do not by themselves establish a random-network theorem.

New paper-only material: Proposition 9, Corollary 10, Example 11 and the weighted-projection notes after Proposition 13 have no Lean coverage in this package. Renumbering the unchanged Lean source inventory does not formalize them.

Numerical scope: Section 8 contains numerical illustrations; they are not Lean proofs. Section 9 discusses experimental evidence, Section 10 formalization, and Section 11 limitations.
